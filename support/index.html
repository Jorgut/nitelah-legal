Frequently asked questions

常见问题

How do I unblock an app right now?

现在想用一个被挡住的 App，怎么办？

Open the shielded app and tap Emergency unlock. You return to the Home Screen; tap the app again to open it. The shield stays off for the rest of that night.

打开被挡住的 App，点 紧急解锁。系统会回到桌面，再点一次图标即可进入。当晚剩余时间不会再自动上盾。

How do I revoke Screen Time access?

如何撤销屏幕使用时间权限？

Open iOS Settings → Screen Time and turn the permission off, or delete NITELAH. Restrictions stop immediately and the app will show that they are not active.

打开 iOS 设置 → 屏幕使用时间，关闭授权，或直接删除 NITELAH。限制会立即停止，App 会如实显示“未授权/不生效”。

Does NITELAH read my messages or watch my screen?

NITELAH 会读我的消息或看我的屏幕吗？

No. It only receives the opaque tokens for the apps you pick and asks iOS to shield them. It never reads content inside other apps.

不会。它只拿到你选择的应用的不透明令牌，并请求 iOS 屏蔽这些应用，不读取其他 App 内的内容。

Does NITELAH detect whether I sleep?

NITELAH 会检测我是否睡着吗？

No. It is not a sleep tracker or a medical device. It only applies the restrictions you configure and reports what it can observe.

不会。它不是睡眠监测设备，也不是医疗产品。它只执行你设置的规则，并报告它能观测到的状态。

Where is my data stored?

我的数据存在哪里？

On your device only, in a sandboxed container shared with the system extensions. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

只存在你的设备上，位于与系统扩展共享的沙盒容器中。没有账号，也没有服务器。详见隐私政策。

Why did an app open even though it should be blocked?

为什么本该被挡住的 App 还能打开？

iOS background scheduling is best-effort. Check that Screen Time permission is still granted and that the bedtime schedule is active in the app. If it keeps happening, email us with your iOS version and what you saw.

iOS 的后台调度不保证逐次准时。请确认屏幕使用时间授权仍然有效、App 内日程处于启用状态。如果反复出现，请把 iOS 版本和现象发邮件给我们。

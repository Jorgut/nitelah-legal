1. Overview

1. 概述

NITELAH is a bedtime app that runs locally on iPhone. It helps adults pause chosen recreational apps during a bedtime window they set themselves. The app is local-first: no account, no server operated by us, and no upload of your usage data.

NITELAH 是一款在 iPhone 本地运行的睡前应用限制工具，帮助成年人按自己设定的时间暂停自己选择的娱乐应用。App 采用本地优先设计：不需要账号，不连接我们的服务器，也不会上传你的使用数据。

2. Information processed on device

2. App 在本地处理的信息

  • Screen Time authorization status — used to determine whether restrictions can take effect.
  • Tokens for the apps, categories or web domains you select — generated by Apple's system picker.
  • Bedtime plan and settings — start/end time and weekdays.
  • Local event timestamps — schedule start/end and threshold events, used to show the real state.
  • 屏幕使用时间授权状态——用于判断限制是否能够生效。
  • 你选择的应用、类别或网站令牌——由 Apple 系统选择器生成。
  • 睡前计划与设置——开始/结束时间与星期。
  • 本地事件时间戳——日程开始、结束、阈值触发等，用于显示真实状态。

This information stays on your device, including the App Group container shared by the app and its system extensions. It is not sent to the developer or any third party.

以上信息仅保存在你的设备上（包括 App 与系统扩展共享的 App Group 容器），不会发送给开发者或任何第三方。

3. What we do not collect

3. 我们不收集的信息

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No usage analytics, crash analytics or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No reading of other apps' content, messages, browsing history or screen.
  • No sleep detection and no medical diagnosis.
  • 不要求账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号。
  • 不收集使用分析、崩溃分析或广告标识符。
  • 不集成第三方广告或分析 SDK。
  • 不读取其他 App 的内容、聊天记录、浏览历史或屏幕。
  • 不检测你是否入睡，也不提供医疗诊断。

4. How information is used

4. 信息如何使用

Only to apply the restrictions you configure on your device, show the real state, and let you unlock or adjust them when you choose.

仅用于在设备上执行你设定的限制、展示真实状态，以及在你主动操作时解除或调整限制。

5. Sharing and transfers

5. 共享与跨境传输

We do not share, sell or transfer this information. The app has no backend of our own; apart from Apple system services (such as In-App Purchase, if enabled), it does not communicate over the network.

我们不共享、不出售、不传输上述信息。App 没有自建服务器；除 Apple 系统服务（如 App 内购买，如启用）外，不进行网络通信。

6. Retention and deletion

6. 保留与删除

  • Data remains until you uninstall the app or clear your selection.
  • You can clear the selection, stop schedules in the app, or revoke Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings.
  • Uninstalling removes the app's local data; some system-level settings may remain and should be checked in Settings.
  • 数据保留在你卸载 App 或清除选择之前。
  • 你可以在 App 内清空选择、停止日程，或在系统设置中撤销屏幕使用时间授权。
  • 卸载 App 会删除其本地数据；iOS 可能保留部分系统级设置，需在系统设置中确认。

7. Permissions and revocation

7. 权限与撤销

Screen Time permission is managed by iOS and can be revoked at any time in Settings. Once revoked, restrictions are not active and the app reports that state truthfully.

屏幕使用时间权限由 iOS 管理，你可以随时在系统设置中撤销。撤销后限制不会生效，App 会如实显示“未授权/不生效”。

8. Children

8. 儿童

NITELAH is intended for adult self-management. It is not directed to children and offers no parental monitoring or remote control.

NITELAH 面向成年人自我管理，不面向儿童，也不提供家长监控或远程控制功能。

9. Security

9. 安全

Data is stored only in iOS sandbox-protected local containers. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no server-side exposure; rely on device passcode and encryption if the device is lost or shared.

数据仅存于受 iOS 沙盒保护的本地容器。由于不上传，服务器侧泄露风险不存在；设备丢失或共享时，请依赖系统锁屏与加密。

10. Changes

10. 政策变更

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync or partner features, we will update this policy before release and describe the new data types, purposes and deletion paths.

如果未来版本引入账号、云同步或搭档功能，我们会在发布前更新本政策，并明确说明新增的数据类型、用途与删除方式。
